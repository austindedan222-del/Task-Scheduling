Help & User Guide

Everything you need to know about scheduling your tasks.

1 Add your tasks

Press Add Task on the dashboard or the My Tasks page. Give it a title, an optional description, a deadline, a priority from 1 (lowest) to 10 (highest), and how many minutes you think it will take.

2 Generate your schedule

Open Schedule and the greedy algorithm orders your tasks by urgency — high priority and near deadlines come first. You can Export to PDF to keep a copy.

3 Get reminder emails

The dashboard lists what is Due Soon (next 2 days) and Overdue. Click Email My Reminders Now to send yourself a summary email immediately.

4 Watch your limits

If the tasks due on one day add up to more than 24 hours of work, an overload warning appears so you can move something earlier or trim its estimate.

Step-by-step

  • Create an account — click Create Account. An email address is required because reminder emails are sent to it.
  • Add a task — the add/edit form opens as a popup. Title, deadline, priority (1–10) and duration (minutes) are required; description is optional.
  • Edit or delete — use the Edit and Delete buttons in the task table on the My Tasks page. You only ever see your own tasks.
  • Generate a schedule — the Schedule page ranks every task by a score of priority and days remaining, then lays them out earliest-first so the most urgent work is done first.
  • Export to PDF — the Schedule page can save the generated order as a PDF file.
  • Log out — the Logout button at the bottom of the sidebar ends your session on this device.

Email reminders

  • Right now: click Email My Reminders Now on the dashboard and a summary of your due-soon and overdue tasks is emailed to your account address straight away.
  • In the background: the send_reminders command checks every account for tasks due within 2 days or already overdue and emails each user once. It is meant to be run on a schedule (cron on Linux/macOS, Task Scheduler on Windows) — see the project README for the exact line to use.
  • Nothing to send? If no task is due soon and none is overdue, no email is sent — you will see a message telling you so.
  • No email address? Accounts without an email address are skipped, so make sure your profile has one.

Tips

  • Be realistic about duration — it drives both the schedule and the overload warning.
  • Re-run the schedule after adding or editing tasks so the order stays current.
  • Deadlines are dates (not times); a task becomes overdue the day after its deadline passes.

Still stuck? Add a task and try the dashboard — the system is designed to be explored.
